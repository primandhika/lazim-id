In bab-literasi-perbaikan.md, no sentence makes a table, figure, study, rubric or policy the subject of a human-action verb (for example 'Tabel 1 memperlihatkan', 'Gambar 1 merangkum', 'Kajian ... menemukan', 'Rubrik pengamatan membantu', 'Pendampingan orang tua menunjukkan').
